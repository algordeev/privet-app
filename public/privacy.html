Privet.

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

The short version: Privet does not collect your personal data.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device. There is no Privet server.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. I never see your name or payment details.

Who makes Privet

Privet is made by Aleksandr Gordeev, an independent developer based in Türkiye (“I” in this policy). For the purposes of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), I am the data controller for the little personal data described below. You can reach me at privetapp.help@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone or iPad:

  • lesson progress, results and saved answers, and your review schedule;
  • statistics such as XP, streak, gems and practice history;
  • your choices in the welcome tour (why you’re learning, your level) and display preferences;
  • reminder settings, and whether speaking exercises are turned on;
  • the Bluetooth companion or printer you last connected (an identifier assigned by iOS and the device’s name), its sound level and whether it should reconnect;
  • whether your Privet Plus subscription was active the last time the app checked with Apple.

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. iOS may include it in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your own backup settings. You can erase your learning data at any time in Privet’s Settings → Reset saved lesson & preferences, and all of it by deleting the app.

Subscriptions and purchases

Privet Plus is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple’s Privacy Policy; I do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app asks Apple whether Plus is active and receives only what it needs for that: which plan you have, its purchase and renewal dates and whether it will renew. This stays on your device. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Bluetooth companions and printer

Privet connects to Bluetooth devices only after you tap Connect or Print. It sends your own companion robot or printer what it needs: reactions, short learning text (such as a word, a question or its meaning), the sound level and the images to print. The keychain companion can send your offline review answers back to the app. All of this passes only between your device and your accessory; nothing is sent over the internet. You can withdraw Bluetooth access at any time in iOS Settings.

Reminders, pronunciation and speaking

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; there is no notification server. Russian pronunciation uses the voices built into iOS, which read the text on your device.

Speaking exercises use the microphone only while the speak button is lit. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone; your voice is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the app keeps only whether each answer was right. You can turn speaking off in Privet’s Settings → Speaking, or withdraw microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings.

What Privet does not do

  • It shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell, rent or share personal data.

If you email me

If you write to privetapp.help@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and I use them only to reply to you. The mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which may store messages on servers outside Türkiye. I keep messages only as long as the conversation needs, and I delete them on request.

This website

The Privet website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website, including this page, loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google; see Google’s Privacy Policy. The lesson demo on the website saves its progress only in your browser.

Children

Privet is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Under KVKK (in particular Article 11) and similar laws such as the EU’s GDPR, you may ask whether I process your personal data, ask for a copy, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Because the app keeps your data only on your own device, the only personal data I may hold is email you have sent me. To make a request, write to privetapp.help@gmail.com; I will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

Changes to this policy

If Privet starts handling data differently, I will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
