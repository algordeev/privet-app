Privet Plus

What does Plus include?

Lessons 1–3 are free. Privet Plus opens every lesson, including new ones as they’re added, and gives you unlimited hearts. It’s available monthly or yearly.

I bought Plus but the app doesn’t show it

In Privet, open your Profile → Get Plus and tap Restore at the top of the Privet Plus screen. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Plus works on all your iPhones and iPads with that account.

How do I cancel?

In Privet, open your Profile → Manage Plus → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Privet Plus. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. After cancelling, Plus stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my lessons if Plus ends?

Lessons you’ve already started or passed stay open, so you can always finish what you began. Other lessons from 4 on lock again until you renew.

Lessons

I can’t hear the Russian pronunciation

Privet uses the Russian voice built into iOS. Turn the volume up, and check that a Russian voice is installed: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Russian. Downloading an “Enhanced” voice there makes it sound more natural. Without a voice, every lesson still works with text and a written sound guide.

I ran out of hearts

Each lesson attempt has three hearts, and a wrong answer costs one. When they run out, you can start the lesson again, refill hearts with 20 gems (you earn gems by finishing lessons), or get unlimited hearts with Plus.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check Privet’s Settings → Reminders, and that notifications for Privet are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Privet on your device. Focus modes can also hold them back.

Your progress

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device: there’s no account and no Privet server. It isn’t shared between your devices. iOS includes it in your device backups, so restoring a backup brings it back. Deleting the app erases it.

How do I start over?

Open Privet’s Settings → Local data → Reset saved lesson & preferences. This clears lessons, statistics and preferences on this device; your Plus subscription isn’t affected.

Companion robot and printer

Do I need the robot?

No. The physical companions are optional, and the virtual robot in the app does everything you need. The desk companion is a working prototype and isn’t for sale yet.

My companion or printer won’t connect

  • Connect from Privet’s Settings → Companion or Settings → Printer, not from the iPhone’s Bluetooth settings.
  • Make sure Bluetooth is on and Privet is allowed to use it: Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → Privet.
  • The printer accepts one connection at a time: disconnect it from any other app first.
  • Switch the device off and on, keep it close, and try Connect again.
